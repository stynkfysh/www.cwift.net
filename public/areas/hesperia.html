What I look at in Hesperia

  1. 1

    Many Hesperia lots are big, and some are set up for horses. I compare a house on an acre with other acre-plus sales and count barns, arenas, block walls and RV parking as part of the property.

  2. 2

    Manufactured homes on their own land are common around Hesperia. I check the foundation, year, size and additions, and compare with other manufactured homes, not with stick-built houses down the street.

  3. 3

    Joshua trees can limit what an owner may clear or build, and plenty of lots here have them. I note them from your photos and aerials, along with dirt roads, wells and septic.

What to photograph

Photograph the whole lot from the corners, the block wall and gate, the barn, corrals or RV pad, and any Joshua trees. For a manufactured home, show the skirting, the porch and deck, and any covered patio.

Hesperia questions

Can I put my Hesperia house up for a bail bond?
California courts can accept real property as bail, and the court will want proof of what your Hesperia house is worth. I can appraise the house from your photos and the county record and compare it with nearby high-desert sales. The court and your attorney handle the rest; my job is an honest value.
How do you appraise a manufactured home on an acre in Hesperia?
I compare it with other manufactured homes on similar lots in Hesperia, Oak Hills and Apple Valley, then adjust for size, year, foundation and additions. Send photos of each room, the outside, the land and the data plate, usually inside a cabinet or closet.
