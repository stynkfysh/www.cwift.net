Amador County questions

Mom's house in Jackson is coming to me. Does Prop 19 change the taxes?
It can. Under Prop 19, a child who makes the Jackson house a primary home may keep all or part of the parent's tax base, depending on market value at the transfer. I give you that value; the Amador assessor and your tax advisor apply the rules.
Should we sell our parents' Amador County house as is or fix it first?
That choice is yours. I appraise the house as it stands, sagging porch and original kitchen included, so you can weigh that number against repair bids and the months a fix-up takes. Along Highway 49, plenty of older homes sell without updates.
