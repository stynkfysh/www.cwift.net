Downtown HB / Beachfront questions

How do you appraise a condo near the pier in downtown HB?
I start with sales in the same building or complex, then similar ones nearby. Ocean view, floor level, parking and HOA dues all matter, and so does any special assessment or pending repair the HOA has announced. Send photos plus the HOA's latest dues statement.
We live back east, can you value dad's beach cottage without us flying out?
Yes. A neighbor, property manager or agent can take phone photos of every room, the outside and the street toward the sand. I combine those with public records, permits and nearby sales, and I can value the cottage as of the date your father died or as of today.
