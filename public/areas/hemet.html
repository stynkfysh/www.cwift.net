Hemet questions

We inherited mom's Hemet house. Do we need an appraisal for the step-up in basis?
It's the usual way to document it. Heirs generally take a tax basis equal to the home's value at the date of death, and your tax preparer will want that figure supported. I value the house as it stood that day, using Hemet sales from that time.
Can an appraisal help lower the property taxes on my Hemet home?
It can support a decline-in-value appeal, though I can't promise a result. If the market value on the assessor's valuation date was below your assessed value, my appraisal gives you evidence to file with Riverside County. The decision belongs to the county.
When would I get the report for a Hemet home?
Within 24 hours of the moment I have both your payment and your photos, weekends and holidays included. If you're not in a rush, the regular schedule is 5–10 business days at $299 or $449.
