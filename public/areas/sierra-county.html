What I look at in Sierra County

  1. 1

    Downieville and Sierra City homes are often old, built close to the river or into the hillside, with additions over generations. I look at foundations, flood exposure, parking and winter access.

  2. 2

    In Sierra Valley, I check whether a house sits on a small homesite or a big ranch parcel with irrigated meadow and barns, since ranch land sells on different terms.

  3. 3

    Older cabins here often rely on wood heat, small septic systems and roofs that have carried decades of snow. I look closely at those, since repairs this far up the canyon cost more and take longer.

Across Sierra County

Sierra County splits into the river canyon towns along the North Yuba and the open ranch country of Sierra Valley. I treat them as separate markets and widen into neighboring counties only when local sales can't carry the analysis.

Sierra County questions

Do we need our own appraisal for probate on a house in Downieville?
In a Sierra County probate, the court's referee sets the inventory value. My separate appraisal helps the heirs decide whether to sell, keep it or buy each other out, and documents the value for basis, built from photos anyone in the family can take.
How can you appraise a Sierra County house when hardly anything sells there?
Carefully, and in the open. I use the closest sales I can find, even older ones or a valley away, adjust them for time, river frontage, size and condition, and explain each choice in plain words.
When would I get the report for a Sierra County home?
Within 24 hours of the moment I have both your payment and your photos, weekends and holidays included. If you're not in a rush, the regular schedule is 5–10 business days at $299 or $449.
