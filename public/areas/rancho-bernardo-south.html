What I look at in Rancho Bernardo South

  1. 1

    On streets that burned in 2007, I compare rebuilt homes with other rebuilds, not the 1970s houses next door, and I note the newer roofs, windows and wiring they came with.

Rancho Bernardo South questions

How much is my late father's Rancho Bernardo South house worth for the trust?
I can tell you as of the date he died, which the trustee and your CPA will want for the step-up in basis, and as of today if you plan to sell. Send photos of each room, the roof and the yard, and tell me if it was rebuilt after the 2007 fire.
Do you appraise homes in Oaks North and other 55-plus communities?
Yes. In Oaks North and Seven Oaks I start with sales in the same community, and I need to know what the HOA covers, like the roof, landscaping or exterior paint, since that changes what a buyer takes on. Send the dues and any special assessment with your photos.
