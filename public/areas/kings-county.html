Home appraisals in Kings County, by tomorrow

Every city and town in Kings County, same fee, same 24 hours, written and signed by me.

Kings County is flat, open farm country, and I appraise homes in Hanford, Lemoore, Corcoran and Avenal and the farmhouses on country roads between them. Hanford has older blocks around its courthouse square and newer tracts at the edges, and Lemoore has newer subdivisions with the naval air station to the west. When a parent dies or a Hanford house has to be divided, I value it from your photos.

What I look at in Kings County

  1. 1

    In Hanford's older blocks near downtown, homes run from early 1900s houses to 1940s cottages. I look at how much of the original house is left and whether the updates suit it.

  2. 2

    Country homes here often sit beside dairies, cotton or orchards. I treat a nearby dairy as part of the location, check the well and septic, and compare with other rural homesites.

  3. 3

    Near Corcoran and the old Tulare Lake bed, flood history matters, especially since the lake came back in 2023. I check the maps and ask what happened, because low ground can outweigh square footage.

Across Kings County

Across Kings County, I separate town lots in Hanford and Lemoore from farmhouses on country roads and from Avenal and Kettleman City on the west side. Flat ground hides real differences in water, flooding and what's next door.

Kings County questions

How fast can I get our Hanford house appraised for our divorce?
I email the report within 24 hours once the fee is paid and your photos are in. I stay neutral, so either spouse or both attorneys can order, and I value the house as of the date the case calls for, current or past.
Is it worth appealing my Kings County property taxes if values dropped?
That depends on how far the market value on January 1 sits below your assessed value. I give you that value with the sales behind it; you decide whether to file. Kings County's regular filing window closes earlier than many, so check the date.
When would I get the report for a Kings County home?
Within 24 hours of the moment I have both your payment and your photos, weekends and holidays included. If you're not in a rush, the regular schedule is 5–10 business days at $299 or $449.
