North Park / University Heights questions

Should we get an appraisal before building an ADU off our North Park alley?
It can help you plan. I'll value the house as it stands today, which gives you a clear starting point, and I can value it again once the unit is finished and permitted. In North Park, sales of bungalows with back units help show what buyers pay for that second home.
What do you need from me to appraise Dad's University Heights house for probate?
The address, the date of death, and photos of every room, the outside and the back of the lot, including any canyon view over Mission Valley or alley unit. Add the attorney's contact if you have one, and I'll value the University Heights house as of the date of death, plus today if the estate needs both.
