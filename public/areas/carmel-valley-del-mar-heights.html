Carmel Valley / Del Mar Heights questions

Can we get our Carmel Valley house appraised for the divorce without both of us there?
Yes. I work from photos and records, so nobody has to schedule a visit or be in the house together, and I compare the home with Carmel Valley resales of the same floor plan, whichever of you pays. Either spouse can send photos, and the other can add what I missed.
We bought in Pacific Highlands Ranch near the top. Can an appraisal lower our taxes?
Only if your Pacific Highlands Ranch home was worth less on January 1 than its assessed value. I value it as of that date using sales of similar homes there and nearby, and if it comes out higher, I'll tell you; either way, Mello-Roos is a separate special tax that an appeal doesn't change.
