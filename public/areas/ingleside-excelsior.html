I value homes here after a parent dies, in divorces and for buyouts between siblings, and since many of these houses haven't changed hands in decades, a solid number matters for taxes and for fairness.

Ingleside / Excelsior questions

Does the in-law unit downstairs add value to our Excelsior house if it isn't permitted?
Some, but less than a legal unit. Buyers here know these rooms well and price in the risk of an order to remove it or the cost of legalizing it. I look for nearby sales with similar setups and say plainly in the report how I treated it.
My brother wants to buy me out of our parents' house in Ingleside, what's fair?
A fair starting point is a market value both of you can see support for. I appraise the house as is, using Ingleside and Excelsior sales, and you each get the same report. How you split costs, credits or rent from a downstairs unit is between you and your attorney.
