What I look at in San Marcos

  1. 1

    In San Elijo Hills, I compare your floor plan with resales in the same neighborhood and note Mello-Roos, HOA dues and views toward Double Peak or the coast from the higher streets.

  2. 2

    Around Lake San Marcos, I separate lakefront and golf course lots from homes a few streets back, and I compare condos there with sales in the same complex.

  3. 3

    In Twin Oaks Valley, acreage, usable land, outbuildings and the access road can matter as much as the house, so I look for other larger-lot sales to compare.

What to photograph

Photograph the view from your highest window, the backyard slope, and any solar panels. Near Lake San Marcos, show the water or fairway from the house; in Twin Oaks Valley, the land, outbuildings and driveway.

San Marcos questions

My ex and I disagree on what our San Marcos house is worth. Can you help?
Couples call me for exactly that. I'm neutral, and I show every San Marcos sale I used and each adjustment, so both of you can see how I got there. In San Elijo Hills that often means resales of your exact floor plan, which makes the comparison easy to follow.
Can my parents transfer their Lake San Marcos home to me and keep the tax base?
Under Prop 19 that depends mostly on whether you'll live at the lake as your main home and on its market value at the transfer. I can give you that value, using lakefront or golf course sales if that's what you have. The assessor decides, and your attorney or CPA can walk you through the filing for the lake house.
