Murrieta sits where the 15 and 215 come together, and its homes run from older streets near the historic downtown to 1990s and 2000s tracts along Murrieta Hot Springs Road and rural acreage up in La Cresta on the Santa Rosa Plateau. Divorces and estates here often turn on one number, and I can get it to you without a site visit.

What I look at in Murrieta

  1. 1

    La Cresta lots are acreage, many with horse setups and long views. I compare them with other Santa Rosa Plateau parcels, not valley tracts, and check the parcel record for size and access.

  2. 2

    Homes along Murrieta Creek and in low spots near it can sit in a flood zone. I check the flood map and note it, since it can change what buyers pay and what they insure.

  3. 3

    West-side homes against the hills often have views and bigger lots, while tracts toward French Valley are newer. I keep comparables on the same side of town when sales allow.

What to photograph

Photograph the view from the yard, the slope and any retaining walls, the pool and the solar panels. On La Cresta acreage, add the barn, arena, fencing, any well, and the drive up to the house.

Murrieta questions

Does the casita or ADU on our Murrieta lot count as extra square footage?
Not as part of the main house. I value a detached ADU or casita on its own line, based on what buyers here pay for one, and I check whether it's permitted. A permitted unit with a kitchen usually counts for more than a bonus room.
My spouse wants to keep our Murrieta house. How do we get a fair buyout number?
Start with an independent market value you can both see. I appraise the house as of the date your attorneys choose, show the Murrieta sales I used and explain each adjustment. The buyout math itself belongs to you and your attorneys.
When would I get the report for a Murrieta home?
Within 24 hours of the moment I have both your payment and your photos, weekends and holidays included. If you're not in a rush, the regular schedule is 5–10 business days at $299 or $449.
