What I look at in Nevada County

  1. 1

    West county homes often sit on wooded acreage with a well, septic and sometimes NID ditch water. I note which you have, since irrigation and usable pasture matter around Penn Valley and Rough and Ready.

  2. 2

    In Lake Wildwood and Lake of the Pines, the gate, the HOA and lake frontage drive value. I compare inside each community first and adjust for waterfront, golf-course lots and docks.

  3. 3

    Many Victorians in Nevada City and Grass Valley have been reworked several times. I look at foundations, old wiring and how additions tie in, and compare them with other older homes.

Across Nevada County

Nevada County divides between the oak and pine foothills around Penn Valley and Grass Valley and the mountain country of Truckee and Donner Summit. I never compare across the two halves; each gets its own sales and its own adjustments.

Nevada County questions

Does a granny unit on our Grass Valley acreage add value?
Usually, if it's permitted and livable. I look for western Nevada County sales with second units, then adjust for size, kitchen, separate entrance and septic capacity. An unpermitted conversion still gets described, but I value it carefully.
Our parents' trust holds a house in Nevada City. Does the trustee need an appraisal?
Usually, yes. It documents the date-of-death value for the step-up in basis and gives everyone a fair figure whether the house is sold or kept. I value the Nevada City house as of the date of death.
When would I get the report for a Nevada County home?
Within 24 hours of the moment I have both your payment and your photos, weekends and holidays included. If you're not in a rush, the regular schedule is 5–10 business days at $299 or $449.
