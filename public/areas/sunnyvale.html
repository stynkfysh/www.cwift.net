What to photograph

For an Eichler, photograph the atrium if you have one, the glass walls, the ceiling beams and the roof. For any house, show the kitchen and baths, the back yard, and the ADU or garage conversion inside and out.

Sunnyvale questions

We added a permitted ADU to our Sunnyvale house, how do you value it?
I look for Sunnyvale and nearby sales with a similar permitted unit and compare size, separate entrance, kitchen and parking. If there aren't enough, I study the rent the unit could bring and how buyers have treated it, and I explain the method plainly in the report.
Mom's Sunnyvale Eichler is all original, is it worth more or less as is?
It depends on what original means. An untouched Eichler with sound radiant heat and a good roof can draw strong interest, while leaking floor pipes, a failing roof or old wiring pull value down. I compare it with Eichler sales in similar condition so the answer comes from the market.
