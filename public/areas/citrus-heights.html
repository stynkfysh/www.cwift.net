What I look at in Citrus Heights

  1. 1

    Bigger lots near Auburn Boulevard and Old Auburn Road can hold older homes, RV parking or a second structure. I compare those with other large-lot sales, not the standard tract houses nearby.

  2. 2

    Within a single tract, houses often differ mostly in updates, so I look at the kitchen, baths, roof, HVAC and windows. A redone kitchen near Sunrise Mall can outsell an original one two streets away.

  3. 3

    Homes backing onto Arcade Creek or busy roads like Greenback Lane and Antelope Road get adjusted for location. I use your photos and a map to see what sits behind the back fence.

What to photograph

Photograph the converted garage or added room, the original or remodeled kitchen, the baths, the pool and its equipment, the roof, and the label on the HVAC unit. Show what's behind the back fence, whether that's Arcade Creek, a busy road or a neighbor.

Citrus Heights questions

Will an unpermitted garage conversion hurt the value of our Citrus Heights house?
It can affect it. I describe the conversion and value the space the way buyers here treat it, often below permitted living area, while noting the lost garage parking. Plenty of Citrus Heights tracts have similar conversions, so I can usually find sales that show the effect.
Can an appraisal help me appeal my property taxes in Citrus Heights?
It can when a Citrus Heights home's market value has dropped below its assessed value, which happens most often after a recent purchase. I appraise the house as of January 1, the assessor's lien date, using sales from around then. The appeal itself is between you and Sacramento County, and I can't promise a result.
