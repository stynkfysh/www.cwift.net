What I look at in North Long Beach

  1. 1

    Condition varies more than size here. I look closely at the roof, windows, electrical panel and plumbing in your photos, since an untouched original house and a remodeled one on the same block can sell far apart.

  2. 2

    Garage conversions, back houses and rooms added over the decades are common. I match the recorded size against what your photos show and give unpermitted space less weight, explaining how I handled it.

  3. 3

    Homes along Artesia Boulevard, Atlantic Avenue or near the 91 and 710 freeways get more noise than quiet streets by the parks. I match those locations with similar sales instead of averaging them together.

North Long Beach questions

What's my late dad's house in North Long Beach worth, and how do I find out?
An appraisal as of the day he died is the usual answer for an inherited house, because that value generally sets your new tax basis and helps divide the estate. Send me photos of the house, the yard and any back unit, and I'll compare it with nearby sales of similar 1940s and 1950s homes.
Can I get a quick appraisal in North Long Beach before listing our house?
Yes. Send photos and payment, and I'll have the report in your inbox within 24 hours, weekends included. An independent value before you list helps you set a price you can defend instead of guessing from what the remodeled house down the street sold for.
When would I get the report for a North Long Beach home?
