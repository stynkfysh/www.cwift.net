Alameda County questions

Do I need my own appraisal for probate in Alameda County?
Not always. A probate referee values the estate's assets for the court, and my report doesn't replace that. Executors from Oakland to Livermore still hire me for an independent date-of-death value to support the step-up in basis or settle a buyout.
Our Oakland condo is assessed above what it would sell for, can an appraisal help?
It can. If you bought near a peak, the assessor can lower the assessment while the market is down, and my report shows sales from your building as of January 1. Alameda County's 2026 appeal deadline was September 15.
When would I get the report for a Alameda County home?
