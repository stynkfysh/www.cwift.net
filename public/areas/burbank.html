What I look at in Burbank

  1. 1

    In the Rancho, many lots are zoned for horses, and a barn, stalls or a riding ring change which sales I compare with. I look for them in your photos and check the lot size on record.

  2. 2

    Homes near Hollywood Burbank Airport hear the planes, and the noise changes street by street. I compare them with sales that share the same exposure rather than with quiet blocks up near the hills.

  3. 3

    Above Glenoaks, foothill homes sit on sloped lots with valley and city views and brush behind them. I weigh the view against the steep driveway, the retaining walls and the brush clearance the hillside needs.

What to photograph

Show the house from the street and the full backyard. In the Rancho, photograph the barn, corrals and tack room. In the hills, show the view, the slope, the steps and the cleared brush, plus any garage conversion or back unit.

Burbank questions

Will our new backyard ADU in Burbank raise the appraised value?
Usually, if it's permitted and finished, though not always by what it cost to build. I compare your house with Burbank sales that also have a second unit, noting its entrance, kitchen and parking. A Burbank garage converted without permits gets a much more careful look.
Do I need an appraisal for Prop 19 if I move into Mom's house?
It's not always required, but it helps. Under Prop 19, a child who moves into a parent's home can keep all or part of the parent's assessed value, depending on the home's market value on the transfer date. My appraisal as of that date supports you if the assessor's figure looks high.
