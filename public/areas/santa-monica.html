What I look at in Santa Monica

  1. 1

    North of Montana, many original homes have been replaced, so lot size and what's on it both matter. I compare original houses and new builds separately and look closely at the lot.

  2. 2

    In Ocean Park, small Craftsman houses and beach cottages sit close to Main Street and the sand, and many were added onto. I sort out the original house from later rooms using the records and your photos.

  3. 3

    Santa Monica's rent control shapes the value of duplexes and small buildings. A unit with a long-term tenant is worth something different from a vacant one, so I ask for the rents and leases.

What to photograph

Photograph the house and lot from the street, the yard and any guest house or back unit. In Ocean Park, show where additions meet the original cottage. For a condo, add any ocean view and your parking space.

Santa Monica questions

Can I keep Mom's old tax base on her Santa Monica house under Prop 19?
Possibly, if you make it your main home. But a long-held Santa Monica house is often worth far more than its assessment, and under Prop 19 value beyond the old assessment plus a set amount gets added. I can document that market value as of the transfer date.
Dad died last spring, can you appraise his Santa Monica condo as of then?
Yes. I set the value as of his date of death, using sales from his building and nearby ones that closed around then. If you've already cleared or repainted the unit, tell me what changed so I describe it as it was. That value usually becomes the heirs' tax basis.
