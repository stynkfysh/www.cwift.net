Riverside County questions

Is there a deadline to appeal property taxes in Riverside County this year?
Yes. For 2026, the county's deadline to file a regular assessment appeal is November 30. What I add is an independent appraisal as of the date your appeal concerns, so the appeals board sees a supported value rather than a guess.
Do I need my own appraisal if mom's house is going through probate?
A California probate uses a court-appointed referee to set the inventory value. Executors often want their own appraisal anyway, to price the house or plan a buyout among siblings. Whether it's in Banning or Blythe, I can value it as of the date of death.
When would I get the report for a Riverside County home?
Within 24 hours of the moment I have both your payment and your photos, weekends and holidays included. If you're not in a rush, the regular schedule is 5–10 business days at $299 or $449.
