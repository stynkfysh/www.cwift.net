Butte County questions

When is the deadline to appeal our property taxes in Butte County this year?
For 2026, the deadline to file an assessment appeal in Butte County is November 30. A decline-in-value appeal looks at your home's worth on January 1, so I appraise it as of that date using Chico, Oroville or Ridge sales. I can't promise the outcome.
How do I find out what my parents' house in Chico is worth?
Send me the Chico address and photos of every room, the outside and the yard. I check the county record and past listings, compare the house with sales from the same part of Chico, and value it as of today or the date your parent died.
When would I get the report for a Butte County home?
Within 24 hours of the moment I have both your payment and your photos, weekends and holidays included. If you're not in a rush, the regular schedule is 5–10 business days at $299 or $449.
