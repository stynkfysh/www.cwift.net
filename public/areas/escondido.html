What I look at in Escondido

  1. 1

    In Old Escondido, I look at original details, foundation and wiring updates, and the historic district rules, then compare with other older homes near downtown rather than newer tracts.

  2. 2

    On hillside lots with avocado or citrus groves, I check water source and meters, how much of the land is planted or usable, and whether the grove adds value or just upkeep.

  3. 3

    In Hidden Meadows and Harmony Grove, outside city limits, I check county permits, any HOA, brush clearance on the slopes, and the drive into town, then compare with sales in the same community.

What to photograph

Photograph the grove and slope from the house and from the bottom of the lot, the well or water meters if you have them, and the pool. In Old Escondido, include the porch, wood floors, and any original windows or trim.

Escondido questions

We've all moved out of California. How do we get Dad's Escondido house appraised?
Ask whoever has a key, a neighbor, the gardener, or an agent you might sell with, to walk through with a phone and send me photos of each room, the yard and the grove if there is one. I handle the rest from here, Escondido records and sales included, and email the report to everyone who needs it.
Can you appraise an Escondido house as of the date our mother died?
Yes. I pull Escondido sales from the months around her death and value the house as it stood then. If you've since cleared the grove or redone the kitchen, send older photos too, and your CPA or attorney can take that date-of-death value from there.
