Mariposa County questions

Our house in Mariposa needs a new roof; should we fix it before you appraise?
You don't have to. I appraise the house as it is on the date you choose, worn roof and pine-needle gutters included, and describe the repair need. For an estate or divorce in Mariposa, the as-is value is usually what's needed.
My brother wants to buy me out of our cabin near Yosemite. Where do we start?
Start with a single appraisal that both of you trust. I value the cabin from photos and recent sales between Fish Camp and Midpines, and the report goes to both of you at once. A neutral number makes the buyout talk much easier.
