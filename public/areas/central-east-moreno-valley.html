What I look at in Central / East Moreno Valley

  1. 1

    Moreno Valley Ranch homes sit on curving streets laid out with greenbelts and parks. I note whether a house backs to open space, a park or a busy road, and I keep comparables inside the Ranch when sales allow.

  2. 2

    Newer tracts out east often carry Mello-Roos or HOA charges that many older homes don't. I read the tax bill and compare like with like, so the yearly cost is built into the value.

  3. 3

    Big distribution buildings line parts of the 60 and the east end. I look at what sits behind and beside a house, from warehouses to open hills toward Lake Perris, because buyers notice both.

Often it can. I'm hired to be neutral, not to side with either spouse, and I can value the house as of today or as of your date of separation. Whether it's a Ranch home or a newer tract near Redlands Boulevard, the report shows the sales and adjustments so both sides can follow it.
