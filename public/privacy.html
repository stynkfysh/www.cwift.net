Privacy

What happens to the details and photos you send.

Cwift.net is run by Brian Ward of Brian Ward Appraisal. Your name, email, the property address, the dates, your notes and your photos are used for one thing: getting your appraisal quoted, billed, written and delivered, plus any follow-up questions.

Requests arrive by email, and photos are stored in a private folder used only for appraisal files. Appraisal standards require me to keep a workfile for each report, so material I rely on is kept with it. I don't sell or share your information unless the law or appraisal standards require it.

No ad trackers run here. The hosting provider, Cloudflare, sees routine technical data like IP addresses, which it uses to stop abuse and tally overall traffic.

Questions: contact@cwift.net. Updated 2026-11-13.
