Questions

Straight answers about the 24-hour service. Anything else: contact@cwift.net.

How fast is 24 hours, really?
Your report is emailed no more than 24 hours after I have both your payment and your photos. Saturdays, Sundays and holidays count like any other day.
What do I need to send?
The address, what the appraisal is for, the date you need the value for, and photos of the outside and inside. A few lines about recent updates or problems help too.
Do you come out to the house?
No. That's what makes it a desktop appraisal, and it's why it can be finished in a day.
Will my CPA, attorney or the IRS accept it?
It's a signed appraisal by a California Certified Residential Real Estate Appraiser, prepared under USPAP, the national appraisal standards. I've done desktop appraisals for every purpose on this site, but acceptance is up to whoever receives the report, so check with your advisor if you're unsure.
Can you value a date in the past?
Yes. Most date-of-death, divorce and gift appraisals are for past dates. If the house has changed since then, send older photos or describe what's different.
What does it cost?
Within 24 hours: $675 for a Standard Desktop or $450 for a Basic Desktop. On the regular 5–10 business-day schedule they're $449 and $299.
Standard or Basic?
Both use the same analysis. Standard adds photos, maps, a property description and a market section, which helps when someone else will review the report.
Why can't I call?
Because it would slow things down. Email and the form put everything in writing at once, photos included. Write to contact@cwift.net any time.
How do I pay?
You'll get a secure invoice by email after I've reviewed your request.
Which properties can you appraise?
Single-family homes and condominiums, including townhomes, anywhere in California. Not 2–4 unit buildings, apartment buildings, commercial property or land.
Can I use it to refinance or get a loan?
No. Lenders order their own appraisals.
What if the report gets something wrong about my house?
Tell me. If I misread a feature or the condition, I'll correct it at no charge.

Need a value by tomorrow?

Send the details now. Once you've paid and the photos are in, the report is on its way within 24 hours.
