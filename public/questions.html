Questions

Straight answers about the 24-hour service. Anything else: contact@cwift.net.

How fast is 24 hours, really?
Your report is emailed no more than 24 hours after I have both your payment and your photos. Saturdays, Sundays and holidays count like any other day.
What do I need to send?
The address, what the appraisal is for, the date you need the value for, and photos of the outside and inside. A few lines about recent updates or problems help too.
Do you come out to the house?
No. That's what makes it a desktop appraisal, and it's why it can be finished in a day.
Will my CPA, attorney or the IRS accept it?
It's a signed appraisal by a California Certified Residential Real Estate Appraiser, prepared under USPAP, the national appraisal standards. I've done desktop appraisals for every purpose on this site, but acceptance is up to whoever receives the report, so check with your advisor if you're unsure.
Can you value a date in the past?
Yes. Most date-of-death, divorce and gift appraisals are for past dates. If the house has changed since then, send older photos or describe what's different.
What does it cost?
Within 24 hours: $675 for a Standard Desktop or $450 for a Basic Desktop. On the regular 5–10 business-day schedule they're $449 and $299.
Standard or Basic?
Both use the same analysis. Standard adds photos, maps, a property description and a market section, which helps when someone else will review the report.
Why can't I call?
Because it would slow things down. Email and the form put everything in writing at once, photos included. Write to contact@cwift.net any time.
How do I pay?
You'll get a secure invoice by email after I've reviewed your request.
Which properties can you appraise?
Single-family homes and condominiums, including townhomes, anywhere in California. Not 2–4 unit buildings, apartment buildings, commercial property or land.
Can I use it to refinance or get a loan?
No. Lenders order their own appraisals.
What if the report gets something wrong about my house?
Tell me. If I misread a feature or the condition, I'll correct it at no charge.
How much is my house worth?
That's exactly what an appraisal answers. I study recent sales of similar homes nearby, adjust for the differences, and give you a signed opinion of market value, as of today or any past date you need.
My mom passed away. What value do I need for her house?
Usually its market value on the date she died, because that's generally the heirs' new tax basis. Your tax preparer will confirm. I can value that date even if it was years ago.
Can my spouse and I use the same appraiser?
Yes. I often work for both spouses at once, once both sides have signed off on the date and the photos. That gives you one number to negotiate from instead of two competing ones.
Can you value the house as if the repairs were finished?
Often, yes. Tell me what work is planned when you order, and I'll confirm whether I can do it and how the report will describe it before you're invoiced.
Do I need an appraisal to appeal my property taxes?
It isn't required, but an independent appraisal of the January 1 value is strong evidence for a Prop 8 review with the assessor or a formal appeal to the county board.
Can I use my house for a property bond?
Often. Under Penal Code §1298 the court has to find equity of at least twice the bail, and most courts want a licensed appraiser's report in the bond packet. Check your court's instructions first.
Can we skip the probate referee?
Sometimes. A court can waive the referee for good cause under Probate Code §8903, and then an independent appraisal can supply the value for the inventory. Ask your probate attorney whether it's realistic in your case.
What if I'm donating the house to a charity?
Then a different set of IRS rules applies: a qualified appraisal timed around the donation and a signed Form 8283. Ask me before ordering and I'll tell you whether my report fits.
Is a virtual or online appraisal the same thing?
If a licensed appraiser signs it, a virtual or remote appraisal is what I call a desktop appraisal. An instant online estimate isn't an appraisal at all.
Do you appraise homes in my city?
If it's in California, yes: every county and every ZIP code, at the same fee. The areas page lists the counties, cities and neighborhoods I've written about, but I'm not limited to them.

Send the details now. Once you've paid and the photos are in, the report is on its way within 24 hours.
