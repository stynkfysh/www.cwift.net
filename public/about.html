Hi, I'm Brian Ward

The appraiser behind every Cwift.net report.

I hold license AR036053 from the state's Bureau of Real Estate Appraisers as a California Certified Residential appraiser. I've appraised homes in California since 2004, more than 7,000 of them, and I've testified as an expert witness in litigation, bankruptcy and family law cases.

Cwift.net is my 24-hour desktop service. Clients kept coming to me with a closing, a filing or a court date that couldn't wait a week for the standard schedule, so I set up a way to deliver the same appraisal within 24 hours, every day of the week. The name is a play on “swift.”

Every report is researched and signed by me. No trainees, no outsourcing, and if I misread something about your home, I fix it free.
